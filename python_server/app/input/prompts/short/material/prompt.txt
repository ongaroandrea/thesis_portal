## MATERIAL CHECK

Evaluate only material response. Ignore texture patterns, UVs, lighting direction or intensity, shadows, camera, geometry, composition, render style, and artistic intent.

Use only material evidence:
- highlight spread and sharpness
- reflection sharpness and clarity
- glossy vs diffuse response
- metallic colored reflections vs dielectric white/gray reflections
- specular strength relative to base color
- Fresnel/IOR falloff at grazing angles
- BRDF lobe shape
- opacity, alpha cutouts, transmission, refraction when visible

Do not treat lighting changes, texture changes, or angle-only brightness shifts as material differences.

Silently compare roughness, metallic behavior, specular/Fresnel/IOR, BRDF lobe, opacity, and transmission. Output only in the required schema.
